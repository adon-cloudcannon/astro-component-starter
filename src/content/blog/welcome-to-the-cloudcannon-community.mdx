## Built around what you need

You told us you wanted somewhere to swap implementation tips, talk through technical challenges, and connect with other developers who get what you're trying to achieve. So that's exactly what we've built: a space where you can easily find answers, share what you've learned, and learn from others who are tackling similar projects.

## Share knowledge, solve problems together

Because CloudCannon is deliberately flexible, and open to the tooling and approaches you choose, there are often multiple ways to solve the same problem. With our new community, you’ll have a space to share your solutions, get specific implementation tips from experienced CloudCannon developers, and learn best practices from the community.

![Screenshot of CloudCannon community](https://cc-dam.imgix.net/blog-community-screenshot-rs.png)

Our community aims to bring together anyone who embraces the collaborative spirit of web development: people who believe in learning from each other and building on shared knowledge. Whether you're asking questions, sharing code snippets and frameworks, or showcasing your latest project, you'll find yourself among peers who are genuinely invested in helping each other succeed.

![Screenshot of a post from the CloudCannon Community](https://cc-dam.imgix.net/blog-community-example-2.png)

We've organized everything to make it easy to find what you're looking for. Jump into technical discussions about features you're working with, check out real CloudCannon sites in our project showcases, join our regular developer meetups for deep dives into specific topics, or connect directly with our team to optimize your workflow.

## Who you’ll meet

### Learn from our platform pros

Our community is open to everyone, including CloudCannon Partners and Experts who've built all sorts of amazing things with our platform. They've tackled everything from big enterprise sites to intricate documentation hubs. Whether you're setting up your first build or fine-tuning your content workflows, you'll be able to see how experienced developers are using CloudCannon in the real world and learn from their creative approaches to solving technical challenges.

![Screenshot composite of a post from the CloudCannon Community](https://cc-dam.imgix.net/blog-community-example-1.png)

### Say hello to our team

You'll also see familiar faces from CloudCannon actively participating in the community. Our onboarding specialists are ready to share their best tips for getting set up, our engineers love diving into technical discussions, and our support team is here to help you troubleshoot. Plus, our SSG experts know all the ins and outs of Astro, Hugo, Eleventy, and more — they're here to help you get the most out of your chosen framework.

![Wireframe with icons and CloudCannon team profile photos](https://cc-dam.imgix.net/blog-community-team-image-r.png)

## Join our founding members

We're really excited to welcome everyone who wants to help shape this community. Whether or not you have experience building sites with CloudCannon, we’re looking forward to chatting!
