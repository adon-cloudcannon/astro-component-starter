At CloudCannon, our mission is to make managing and editing websites intuitive, collaborative, and enjoyable. While we offer optional website hosting, our real focus has always been enabling teams to build and edit beautiful websites with ease — and hosting simply provides the foundation that makes this possible. It also powers those live previews we all love 😃

We use Cloudflare’s global CDN to deliver your sites quickly and reliably to audiences worldwide. As more businesses have chosen CloudCannon, our marginal bandwidth costs have decreased. And since you made this growth possible, we’re giving those savings back to you.

---

## More hosting for less

As of today, the following CloudCannon plans will include higher hosting allocations and lower overage costs:

* Standard plan → increased from **75 GB to 110 GB**
* Team plan → increased from **300 GB to 700 GB**

We’re also reducing overage pricing for all currently offered plans from **$20 per 100 GB** to **$15 per 100 GB.**

---

## Host on CloudCannon?

We offer hosting as a nice-to-have convenience for businesses that want the simplicity of managing their content and hosting on one platform. Many of our [Partner agencies](https://cloudcannon.com/partner-program/ "CloudCannon Partner Program") love this convenience, while most of our [enterprise customers](https://cloudcannon.com/enterprise/ "CloudCannon for Enterprise") use their own hosting. You choose what works best for your workflow!

We hope these increased hosting allocations help you focus on your sites and your team, not your bandwidth limits. We’re looking forward to seeing what you continue to build on CloudCannon, and we’re proud to help make our platform more cost-effective.
